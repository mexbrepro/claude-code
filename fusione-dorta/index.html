Join us in 2026

It's early morning. The lake is still, the mountains hold the valley, and somewhere below the terrace someone is preparing cacao. This is Fusione d'Orta — six days above Lago d'Orta where a group of people comes together to breathe, move, cook, rest, and do the inner work that everyday life rarely makes room for.

There is no program to perform and nothing to achieve. There is a rhythm: breathwork in the morning, the lake in the afternoon, ceremony and dance when the evening comes. Some of it will move you deeply. Some of it is simply an Italian summer with good people and very good food.

Both belong.

Location

Centro d'Ompio, above Lago d'Orta

Centro d'Ompio sits on a mountainside above the lake — a retreat house with gardens, a pool, open terraces, and a view that does half the work for us. Rooms range from simple shared spaces to private doubles. The kitchen cooks vegetarian, Italian, generous. Ompio has hosted inner work for decades; the place carries it.

Lago d'Orta, Piedmont, Italy — about an hour from Milan Malpensa airport.

Rhythm

A day at Fusione

Mornings begin with breathwork — Transformational Breath, guided, in a held space. After lunch the day opens: the lake, the sauna, a walk, a conversation, a nap in the garden. Late afternoons bring workshops and ritual work — cacao ceremony, movement, constellation, painting, whatever the week calls for. Evenings we eat long, and some nights we dance until the room is warm.

The week has a form, and the form holds you. Within it, you choose your own pace.

Who it's for

Fusione d'Orta is for people who work with people — facilitators, therapists, teachers, founders — and for anyone who carries a lot and needs a place to set it down. You don't need experience with breathwork or ceremony. You need a week, an open mind, and the willingness to be seen.

Practical

Good to know

How do I get to Centro d'Ompio?

Fly into Milan Malpensa (about an hour away). From there, take the train toward Orta-Miasino, or drive. We organise a shared shuttle from Malpensa and from Orta-Miasino station for the official arrival window — details go out with your confirmation.

What are the rooms like?

Rooms range from simple shared rooms to private doubles, depending on the contribution level you choose. All are simple, clean, and part of the retreat house.

What kind of food is served?

All meals are vegetarian, Italian, and generous, prepared by the Centro d'Ompio kitchen. Let us know about allergies or intolerances when you book.

What should I bring?

Comfortable clothes for breathwork and movement, swimwear for the lake, a journal, and anything that helps you feel at home for a week. A full packing list goes out before arrival.

What is the cancellation policy?

Full refund up to 60 days before arrival, 50% up to 30 days before, and no refund after that — we can usually help you find someone to take your place. Details are confirmed at checkout.

What language is the retreat held in?

English, with Italian and German spoken by parts of the team. The group itself is usually a mix of nationalities.
